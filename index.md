# Publication Impact Analysis in Digital Agriculture

## Overview

This project explores a central question in academic research strategy:

> **What makes a paper get cited more?**

Using publication data from [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/), we analyze the output of 12 leading researchers in digital and precision agriculture from **2018 to 2025** — focusing on their corresponding-author papers to isolate their most direct scientific contributions. The visualizations and trends shown in the notebooks cover **2020 to 2025**, with the 2018–2019 data used to construct two-year citation windows that provide a more robust measure of early citation impact.

***

## Target Authors

The analysis centers on the following researchers, selected for their prominence and interconnectedness in the field:

Ciampitti · Basso · Kaiyu · Grassini · Archontoulis · Castellano · Lobell · Peng · Vara Prasad · Rattalino · Hoogenboom · Messina

***

## Structure of the Analysis

### 1. Target Author Analysis

A focused citation analysis of the 12 target authors. Only papers where these researchers appear as **corresponding authors** are included. The goal is to identify which papers attracted the most citations and what characteristics — timing, topic, journal — they share.

### 2. Topic & Journal Analysis

Using the same set of corresponding-author papers, this notebook shifts the lens from individual authors to **topics and journals**. It examines which subject areas and publication venues are associated with higher citation counts, looking for trends that reveal where scientific attention in digital agriculture is currently concentrated.

### 3. All Authors Analysis

To broaden the picture beyond the 12 target authors, this notebook incorporates data from nearly **50 additional researchers** — co-authors who share at least 30 papers with the target group. This extended network provides a more general view of citation patterns across the field, helping to answer not just what has been cited, but **where it is most strategic to publish and on what topics** going forward.

***

## Data Source

All publication and citation data was retrieved from **[OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/)**, an open and comprehensive index of scholarly works, authors, venues, and citation relationships.
